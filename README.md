#Saving Benny 
              #— The Chase Begins!
Benny spots a squirrel at the dog park and takes off after it. By the time you catch up, all you can find is his leash. Which way did he go?

Saving Benny is a 2D browser game we're designing for our group project at Metropolia University of Applied Sciences. We started with a paper prototype where you play as Benny's owner. Each time the path splits, you decide where to look for him next.
How the game works
You start with three lives. Pick up Benny's leash along the way and keep an eye out for a flashlight. You'll need it when the trail leads into a dark cave. Inside, you catch sight of cavemen chasing Benny and have to decide whether to follow them or try another way around.

A wrong turn can cost you a life. Even when you find Benny, you still have one more decision to make. You win when you both get out safely.
From paper to browser
Our plan is to use HTML and CSS for the scenes, JavaScript for movement, items and choices, and Python with Flask for saving progress. The paper prototype is where we worked out what happens on each route.
